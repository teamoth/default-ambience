Privacy Policy

Default Ambience, by Timbient. Last updated 25 October 2026.

In short: Default Ambience doesn't collect, track or share anything about you. Everything you make stays on your own device. The only time anything leaves your device is when you choose to send feedback.

This policy covers the Default Ambience app for Android and the website at teamoth.github.io/default-ambience.

What stays on your device

  • Saved sessions and settings (such as your chosen theme and MIDI output) are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never uploaded.
  • Session codes are created on your device. They are only shared if you copy one and send it to someone yourself.
  • Sound is generated entirely on your device. Nothing you play is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • MIDI: if you turn on MIDI output, notes are sent only to the MIDI device you choose. The app asks your permission first.

What is never collected

There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies set by the app. It doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Feedback

If you use the Send feedback form, the message you write (and your name or email address, only if you choose to add them) is sent to the developer by email, using the Web3Forms service. The app's version number is included so problems can be fixed. A spam check by hCaptcha runs when you send, and hCaptcha may process technical information such as your IP address for that purpose, under its own privacy policy. Feedback is used only to improve the app and to reply to you if you asked for a reply. It is never sold or shared.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The website stores a copy of itself on your device so it works offline.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) removes everything it stored. To have a feedback message you sent deleted, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
